# Search Console and business listing

1. Open https://search.google.com/search-console/ using the clinic owner's account and add the production domain as a Domain property.
2. Copy Google's TXT verification record into the domain's DNS and verify ownership once it propagates.
3. Check the canonical HTTPS host. Verify that /bn and /en have self-referencing canonicals and reciprocal language links, and robots.txt permits public pages.
4. Open Sitemaps and submit sitemap.xml. Inspect both localized URLs with URL Inspection, test their live versions, and request indexing once approved for launch.
5. Monitor indexing exclusions, Core Web Vitals, impressions, clicks and queries. Recheck after domain or route changes.
6. Have the clinic owner claim or create its Google Business Profile, confirm its map pin and hours, and use the same name, address and telephone as the website. Add the verified map URL to the site and rebuild.

These are owner-side launch steps. Account verification, indexing and map verification are pending.
